# DRE constitution

1. Justify every computation.
2. Preserve information until invalidated.
3. Keep geometry representation-independent.
4. Treat frames as output, not the fundamental computation unit.
5. Account for memory movement.
6. Measure visual error and temporal stability.
7. Make algorithms replaceable.
8. Require reproducible benchmarks.
9. Document failed experiments.
10. Maintain conventional controls.
